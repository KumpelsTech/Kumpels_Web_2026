📬 Q2 2026 at Kumpels: new clients and strategic partnerships

Published on July 13, 2026

New clients, strategic partnerships, and exciting pilots ahead.

This quarter marked another important step in Kumpels' journey.

More than attending events or building new connections, we transformed months of conversations and collaboration into concrete partnerships that will help us bring safer and smarter healthcare to more institutions.

🏥 Vita Fertility becomes a new Kumpels client

We are excited to announce that Vita Fertility has officially joined Kumpels as a client and completed the initial payment for the project.

We are now preparing to launch our pilot together with their team. This implementation will allow us to deploy our technology in a highly specialized fertility care environment while addressing clinical, operational, and administrative challenges previously identified during our joint assessment.

This partnership represents another important milestone in our mission to develop technology tailored to the real needs of healthcare providers.

🤝 We signed a Letter of Intent with Fertility Dreams

We are also pleased to announce the signing of a Letter of Intent (LOI) with Fertility Dreams, marking the beginning of a promising strategic collaboration.

Together, we will explore opportunities to co-develop innovative solutions that improve patient care and operational efficiency within reproductive healthcare.

For Kumpels, this agreement represents the first step toward a long-term partnership built on innovation, collaboration, and shared value.

🚀 A quarter of commercial growth

This quarter reflects an important evolution for Kumpels. We are moving beyond identifying opportunities and turning them into real commercial relationships and strategic partnerships.

The upcoming pilot with Vita Fertility and our new collaboration with Fertility Dreams strengthen our presence in the fertility sector and create new opportunities to validate and scale our technology.

Each new partnership brings us one step closer to our vision of making healthcare safer, smarter, and more efficient.

💊 What does Kumpels do?

We develop technology that improves patient safety, medication traceability, and healthcare operational efficiency.

Our solutions focus on two key areas:

  • Clinical: a Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) that analyzes and validates medical prescriptions in real time, helping healthcare professionals prevent medication errors before they reach the patient.
  • Operational: a platform that enhances medication traceability and streamlines hospital workflows through intelligent automation and real-time visibility.

We work side by side with healthcare institutions to understand their challenges and build solutions that fit their daily operations.
