📬 From invisible to measurable: what we have learned from our clients

Published on March 27, 2026

In healthcare, some of the most costly problems are not the most complex ones: they are the ones no one can see.

For years, medication management in hospitals has relied on fragmented processes, manual records, and validations that occur after the error has already happened.

Not due to a lack of clinical knowledge, but due to a lack of operational visibility.

At Kumpels, we decided to address this problem at its core.

What did we learn after the pilot?

The Farmacia On Time pilot in a high-complexity hospital in Colombia brought us great learnings.

For the first time in the institution, the full medication workflow was continuously tracked:

  • Pre-dispatch
  • Preparation
  • Pharmaceutical validation
  • Delivery
  • Returns

The pilot involved over 170 clinical and operational users, generating real data on process times, errors, rework, and operational behavior.

The most important finding was not technological, but structural.

Most operational costs do not come from clinical errors, but from everything that happens around the process:

  • Incomplete records
  • Manual reconciliations
  • Administrative rework
  • Lack of traceability between teams

In other words: the problem is not what happens, it’s that it cannot be seen in time.

Data & Evidence

From the pilot, we were able to quantify this impact:

  • 88% reduction in time spent on administrative and operational rework.
  • Over 90% reduction in rework caused by errors, thanks to early interception in pharmacy.
  • COP $167 million in direct annual operational savings.
  • 128% ROI in the first year, even with partial adoption.

Additionally, when translating operational impact into clinical terms, delays associated with rework decreased from 120 days to 24 days per year, representing a significant improvement in care timeliness and patient safety.
